# Isolation Guidelines

## How long should I isolate?

If you test positive for covid, general public-health guidance
recommends isolating at home for at least 5-7 days from the start of
symptoms, or from your test date if you have no symptoms. You should
stay isolated until you have been fever-free for 24 hours without
using fever-reducing medicine, and your other symptoms are improving.

## Who should isolate

Anyone who tests positive should isolate, even with mild or no
symptoms, since covid can spread before symptoms appear. Household
members who were in close contact should monitor themselves for
symptoms for 5-10 days.

## Isolation setup at home

Use a separate room and bathroom if possible. Keep the room
well-ventilated - open windows when weather allows. Avoid sharing
personal items like towels, utensils, and bedding with other
household members. Wear a mask when you must be in shared spaces.

## Ending isolation

Isolation can generally end once fever has resolved for 24 hours
(without medicine) and other symptoms are improving. If you had a
more severe illness, or a weakened immune system, isolate longer and
consult your doctor before resuming normal activities.
