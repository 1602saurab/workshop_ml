# Home Care Guidance

## General care while recovering at home

Rest as much as possible and stay hydrated - water, oral
rehydration solutions, and clear soups all help. Eat light,
nutritious meals as your appetite allows. Monitor your temperature
and symptoms once or twice a day and note any changes.

## Managing fever and discomfort

Over-the-counter fever and pain relief medicines can help with
discomfort, but the right medicine and dose depends on your age,
weight, and any existing health conditions. Always follow the
label instructions or your doctor's/pharmacist's advice rather than
a general guideline - this tool does not provide specific medicine
dosages, since that needs to be personalized by a qualified
professional.

## Monitoring at home

If you have a thermometer, track your temperature daily. If you have
a pulse oximeter, you can track oxygen saturation - a reading that
drops below 94%, or is trending downward, is a reason to contact a
doctor promptly (see "When To See A Doctor").

## Supporting recovery

Gentle breathing exercises, adequate sleep, and gradually returning
to activity (rather than all at once) are commonly recommended once
symptoms start improving. Avoid strenuous exercise until you have
fully recovered, since pushing too soon can slow recovery.
