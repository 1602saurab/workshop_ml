# Testing And Recovery

## Types of tests

RT-PCR tests are the most accurate and are typically used to confirm
a diagnosis. Rapid antigen tests give faster results (within
15-30 minutes) but can occasionally miss an infection, especially
early on - a negative rapid test with strong symptoms is sometimes
followed up with a PCR test.

## What a "positive" screening result from this clinic means

This clinic's screening tool estimates covid risk based on reported
symptoms (age, fever, cough, and other factors) using a trained
model. It is a triage aid, not a diagnostic test. A "Positive" result
means the model estimates elevated risk based on the symptom pattern
- it does not confirm infection. Confirm with an actual PCR or
antigen test, and consult a doctor for next steps.

## Recovery timeline

Most people with mild-to-moderate covid recover within 1-2 weeks.
Some people experience lingering symptoms (fatigue, mild
breathlessness, or reduced sense of taste/smell) for several weeks
afterward - this is common and usually improves gradually. If
lingering symptoms are severe or not improving after a month, consult
a doctor.

## After recovery

Re-infection is possible, so continue sensible precautions (hand
hygiene, masks in crowded indoor spaces during local outbreaks,
staying home if unwell) even after recovering once.
